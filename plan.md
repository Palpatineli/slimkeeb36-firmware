1. PCB: kicad then order from pcbway
2. MCU: [supermini NRF52840](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256805848952479.html)
3. battery: [big battery](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3898), 400mah, 36x17x7.8mm
    [small battery](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256807334111964.html)
4. firmware: it's compatible with zmk
5. switches: [kailh choc v1 pro pink](https://lowprokb.ca/products/kailh-choc-low-profile-switches)
6. keycap: [MBK PBT keyset](https://keeb.io/products/mbk-pbt-blank-keyset?variant=40373454045278)
7. case: laser cut aluminium/acrylic sendcutsend
8. header: [low profile header](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/mill-max-manufacturing-corp/315-43-114-41-001000/4455235)

# misc

The supermini board can be attached up-side down to the main board with samtec PHT. PHT has a tolerance of 0.889 ~ 1.194mm for the pin hole. It should be safe to drill a pin hole with a diameter of 1.1mm
